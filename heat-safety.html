Heat Safety in Phoenix

Phoenix experiences extreme summer temperatures. Understanding heat risks can help residents prepare and stay safe.

Basic Heat Safety Tips

  • Drink water regularly throughout the day.
  • Limit strenuous outdoor activities during extreme heat.
  • Rest in shaded or air-conditioned spaces.
  • Check on neighbors, older adults, and others at risk.

Recognizing Heat Illness

Heat exhaustion may cause heavy sweating, dizziness, weakness, or nausea. Heat stroke is a medical emergency. If heat stroke is suspected, call 911.

Recognizing Heat-Related Illness

This table summarizes warning signs and actions to take when someone experiences heat illness.

Heat illness symptoms and recommended actions
Condition Warning Signs Recommended Action
Heat Cramps Muscle pain or spasms and heavy sweating. Stop activity, move somewhere cool, and drink water.
Heat Exhaustion Dizziness, weakness, nausea, or heavy sweating. Move to a cool place, loosen clothing, and seek medical help if symptoms worsen.
Heat Stroke Confusion, altered mental state, or loss of consciousness. Call 911 immediately and begin cooling the person.
